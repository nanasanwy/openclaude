How to test

Run a Saturday at the gate in ten minutes

This is the real access software running in this page with test data and a simulated clock. Nothing here touches StoreHub, Umai or real gates. Your test data stays in this browser until you press Reset test data.

Use Tablet + simulator side by side on a laptop, or switch tabs on a phone. Bands are typed into the scan boxes and Enter pressed, exactly what a USB scanner does.

1. A walk-in family arrives

  1. Staff tablet: log in as Cashier 2222 → Activate bands.
  2. Enter receipt SH-10442, table 12 → Start group & scan bands.
  3. Scan A-000101, K-000101, K-000102. The prefix tags each band as adult or kid.
  4. Gate simulator: press Scan IN on all three. The 3-hour timer starts at the first scan.

2. The kids exit rule

  1. Scan K-000101 OUT on its own: red, Adult must scan out first.
  2. Scan A-000101 OUT, then the kid within a minute: both open.
  3. Wait more than a minute (or press +5 min) and try the other kid alone: refused again.

3. Overtime and the exit lock

  1. Scan everyone back IN. Re-entry is allowed and the timer keeps running.
  2. Move the clock forward past 3 hours and 15 minutes from entry (+1 hour three times, +15 min, +1 min).
  3. Scan the adult OUT: Please see our host.
  4. Log in as Supervisor 4444 → Live dashboard. The group shows the blocks due and the amount in RM. Open it → Overtime paid — clear with any receipt number. Now the family can leave.

4. A birthday party with a QR invite

  1. Log in as Events 5555 → Party setup. Create a party; the e-invite with its QR code opens.
  2. Log in as Reception 3333 → Party check-in. Type the 6-letter code (a counter scanner reads the QR into the same box) and scan a few bands such as A-300001, K-300001.
  3. Party bands only open the gate during the party time, with no grace period.

5. Packages, safety and reports

  1. Cashier → Packages: the demo account is on phone 0123334444 with 12 visits. On Activate bands, use Use visit package… to deduct visits per kid.
  2. Simulator → Trigger fire alarm: both gates open and every rule is ignored. Reset it after.
  3. Supervisor → Handheld gate scan is the backup when a reader fails; Open once and Hold open are on the dashboard. Every override asks for a reason and is logged with the staff name.
  4. Manager 1111 → Reports for the day's visitors, overtime to match against StoreHub, overrides and package use; Settings for every rule and price.

What the test drive can't do. Downloads (CSV reports, the invite image) and printing are blocked inside this preview page. They work on the venue PC. Real gates, readers and the fire panel connect through the lane controller on site.
